Security notes and limitations
  • Uses AES-GCM authenticated encryption and a passphrase-derived key (PBKDF2-SHA-256).
  • The passphrase is never included in the exported payload or saved by this page.
  • This static demo does not host, deliver, expire, or revoke notes. You share the encrypted payload yourself.
  • A weak or reused passphrase can put a note at risk. Share the passphrase through a different channel.
  • This has not had an independent security audit. Do not use it for highly sensitive or life-critical information.
  • Browser extensions, malware, screenshots, clipboard history, and compromised devices can expose text before encryption or after decryption.
A small privacy engineering experiment. No account, analytics, or server-side note storage is implemented in this version. The browser may still retain clipboard contents or downloaded files.
